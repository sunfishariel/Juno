_Module 5. Generated by `M5 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html`. Paste over `05-evals/human-rubric.md`._

## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno's prioritized risk list.
- **Value proposition:** Turn inconsistencies in documents into a cited and prioritized list that I can revise off of, based on previously established product strategy goals.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hallucinations (deal-breaker) | Does the list present inconsistencies that truly exist in each document?  | Yes / No | Not all of the inconsistencies actually exist, across documents.  | n/a | All inconsistencies actually exist, across documents document.  |
| The priority of inconsistencies align with the goals of the product strategy.  | _ | 1 to 5 | None of the prioritized list align with the goals.  | Some align with the goals / all align with the goals but are in the wrong order.  | All of list is aligned with and ordered correctly based on the goals.  |
| Tone | Are the risks written in a comprehensive way? | 1 to 5 | I don't understand it at all.  | I don't like the tone, but I understand it. | I like the tone, and I understand it.  |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** Randomly select 20 answers to score per week.
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, and my lead engineer.
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** When >25% of the answers have different scores from each of us, we will revise the wording of our rubric. In particular, we will focusing on wording that might be confusing to each of our backgrounds (e.g. non-technical vs. technical).

## Pass bar

Every yes/no question must pass. Every 1-5 question should receive a score of >=3.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Hallucinations: any "no"
- No claimed information accessed outside of the provided documents. No profanity or hostility in written tone.
